Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

?Do kids need prior experience?+
None. We start with the fundamentals. Many of our most successful robotics students started knowing nothing about engineering.
?What do they take home?+
In the summer camp, every kid takes their 3D-printed robot home after competition day. In year-round classes, students keep their builds and projects.
?Is this more engineering or coding?+
Both equally. Kids learn CAD design, 3D printing, and physical assembly alongside programming robot behavior. It's our most complete STEM experience.
?Is there a summer camp?+
Yes — Sumo Bots: Build & Battle runs July 20–24, 9AM–12PM at Plainsboro Recreation. One of our most popular camps every year.
?What ages?+
Ages 6–14. We have beginner tracks for younger kids and more advanced engineering challenges for older students.
